Two improvements to wrap():
1. Keep paragraph breaks. A blank line (a line containing only whitespace) separates paragraphs; in the output, paragraphs are separated by a single "" entry. Several blank lines in a row still give just one "". No leading/trailing "" entries.
2. Never split URLs (words starting with http:// or https://). If a URL is longer than width, put it on a line of its own instead of chunking it.
