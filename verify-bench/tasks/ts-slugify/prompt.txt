slugify drops accented letters entirely, so "Crème Brûlée" becomes "crme-brle". Make it transliterate accented Latin letters to their ASCII base (é→e, ü→u, ñ→n, and also ß→ss, æ→ae, ø→o, œ→oe), and make sure emoji and other symbols are simply dropped. Also, underscores in the input should be treated as word separators (so "foo_bar baz" gives "foo-bar-baz", or "foo_bar_baz" with separator "_").
